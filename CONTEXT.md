# Airebound

Airebound is a one-day game-jam project about surviving and taking risks in dangerous air. This glossary defines the game's player-facing concepts and keeps the design language consistent.

## Game concepts

**Balloon**:
The player's light geometric vessel, carried by and reacting to the air around it.
_Avoid_: Player character, ship, avatar

**Air Current**:
A directed movement of air that changes how the Balloon travels through the room.
_Avoid_: Wind zone, stream

**Turbulence**:
An unstable Air Current that makes the Balloon harder to control and increases danger.
_Avoid_: Storm, random hazard

**Gust**:
A deliberate burst of air produced by the Balloon to create space and alter its movement.
_Avoid_: Attack, weapon, blast

**Hazard**:
Any environmental force or object that can end a Run when it collides with the Balloon.
_Avoid_: Enemy, obstacle

**Near Miss**:
A narrowly avoided collision with a Hazard that demonstrates the Balloon was in danger.
_Avoid_: Combo, dodge bonus

**Risk Multiplier**:
The score multiplier earned by remaining close to danger and performing Near Misses.
_Avoid_: Combo meter, streak

**Run**:
One attempt that begins when the Balloon enters the room and ends when a Hazard collides with it.
_Avoid_: Round, level, match

**Escape Route**:
A readable path through the room that lets the Balloon avoid an approaching Hazard.
_Avoid_: Safe spot, exit
