# Airebound — itch.io submission

## Title

Airebound

## Short description

Survive dangerous air currents, dodge hazards, and master your Gust in this neon arcade survival game.

## Description

The room is moving. Steer your balloon through shifting air currents, dodge turbulence, debris, and pressure vents, then use a timed Gust to make space when the danger closes in. Survive longer, take bigger risks, and climb the shared online arcade leaderboard.

Built for the 1-Day Jam #2 theme: AIR.

## Controls

- Arrow keys or WASD — steer
- Space — release a Gust
- L — open the online arcade leaderboard
- Space after failure — retry
